# P5R Android 0.5.13 — NCE

## New in this build

- **Refreshed launcher:** Persona 5-inspired red-and-black styling, updated app icon, cleaner tab labels without numbers, and lightweight screen and selection animations.
- **Launcher personalization:** choose from three backgrounds, enable subtle background motion, toggle the supplied tab-selection sound, and optionally enable haptic feedback.
- **Play History:** see the most recent play session's duration, date, and graphics profile in the launcher.
- **Smart Performance Profiles:** switch among Performance, Balanced, and Quality presets, or use Manual. Returning to Manual restores the saved manual graphics settings.
- **Launcher controller navigation:** use a controller to move focus, select settings, switch launcher pages and subtabs, return to Display, and start the game. Gameplay controller handling is unchanged.
- **Thermal pacing:** optional launcher control lowers the frame cap temporarily when Android reports severe heat (15 fps) and further at critical heat (10 fps), then restores the configured cap as the phone cools. It does not change resolution, textures, or other graphics settings. Performance and thermal behavior depend on the device.

The launcher also retains the existing game settings, including the asynchronous shader-pipeline option.
