Persona 5 Royal.

An Android port project that runs the native game code on arm64 devices using Vulkan. The app contains the engine only: bring your own legally dumped game and keys.

What’s in the launcher

Controller navigation

Navigate the launcher with a controller: move focus, select settings, switch pages, return, and launch. Gameplay input remains handled by the game.

Thermal pacing

Optional pacing lowers the cap to 15 fps at severe heat and 10 fps at critical heat, then restores the configured cap as the device cools. It leaves graphics settings alone.

Smart performance profiles

Choose Performance, Balanced, or Quality, or return to Manual. Manual restores your saved graphics settings.

Play History

See your latest session’s duration, date, and selected graphics profile.

Make the launcher yours

Pick a background, turn on gentle background motion, toggle the tab sound, and optionally enable haptics. The refreshed red-and-black look has lightweight animations and an updated app icon.

Built for Android

Native arm64 execution, Vulkan rendering, touch controls, gamepad support, and on-device shader preparation.

Requirements

64-bit Android device, Android 10 or newer, Vulkan 1.1, and your own legally dumped Persona 5 Royal 1.0.0 game and keys. Performance varies by device; Adreno 610-class GPUs are a low-end target.
